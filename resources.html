Guidance for families

In an emergency, call 911. Home care is not a substitute for emergency medical services.

When is it time for home care?

Changes in personal routines, meals, mobility, housekeeping, isolation, or caregiver stress can signal that extra support may help.

Preparing for a care consultation

Think about the person’s daily routine, preferred schedule, safety concerns, interests, family involvement, and the tasks where help is most needed.

Supporting family caregivers

Respite and shared responsibilities can help family caregivers protect their own time and energy while remaining involved.

Questions to ask a care provider

Ask about assessments, caregiver screening, communication, supervision, scheduling, care-plan changes, and after-hours procedures.

Planning for changing needs

Care needs can evolve. Regular communication helps families adjust schedules and services as circumstances change.
