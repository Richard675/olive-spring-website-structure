Personalized care

The right help can make home feel easier.

Alzheimer’s & Dementia Home Care

Compassionate, specialized care tailored to support cognitive health, safety, and daily routines. Our trained caregivers provide patience, dignity, and familiarity to enhance quality of life for your loved one at home.

Ask about this service: Alzheimer’s & Dementia Home Care

Post-Surgical Care

Expert recovery support in the comfort of your home. We assist with wound care, pain management, mobility, and daily tasks while monitoring healing progress. Our licensed professionals create a safe, personalized plan to support a faster recovery and help prevent complications.

Ask about this service: Post-Surgical Care

Assisted Living Home Services

Supporting independence with dignity. Our caregivers assist with activities of daily living, medication reminders, and companionship while fostering a safe, comfortable environment tailored to your unique needs and preferences.

Ask about this service: Assisted Living Home Services

Private Duty Nursing

Specialized one-on-one medical care at home. Our licensed nurses manage chronic conditions, complex care needs, and recovery with services such as medication administration, IV therapy, and vital-sign monitoring, ensuring safety and comfort around the clock.

Ask about this service: Private Duty Nursing

Chronic Illness Management

Personalized, ongoing care to help manage symptoms, prevent complications, and improve daily life. Our team coordinates with doctors to provide medication oversight, lifestyle guidance, and emotional support, empowering you to thrive at home.

Ask about this service: Chronic Illness Management

Disability Care

Empowering individuals with disabilities to live fully and independently at home. Our caregivers provide tailored support for daily living, mobility, communication, and community engagement, combining practical assistance with dignity, respect, and personalized care plans.

Ask about this service: Disability Care

Adult Care Home

Compassionate, round-the-clock support for adults who need assistance with daily living. Our caregivers help with personal care, meal preparation, household tasks, and social engagement, prioritizing safety, comfort, and meaningful connection in a nurturing home environment.

Ask about this service: Adult Care Home

Non-Medical Home Care

Everyday help that makes life at home easier. Our caregivers provide companionship, assistance with bathing, grooming, and dressing, light housekeeping, meal preparation, errands, and respite for family caregivers, all delivered with warmth, dignity, and respect.

Ask about this service: Non-Medical Home Care

Pediatric Care

Gentle, expert care for children with medical needs. Our pediatric-trained nurses and caregivers provide specialized support for chronic conditions, recovery, or developmental challenges, prioritizing comfort, safety, and a nurturing environment where children can grow and thrive at home.

Ask about this service: Pediatric Care

Service availability and scope depend on assessment, staffing, applicable North Carolina requirements, and the agency’s licensure. This website does not replace medical advice or emergency services.
